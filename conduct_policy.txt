Arrowstack Foods Pvt Ltd - Code of Conduct

1. Employees must respect all colleagues regardless of gender, caste, or religion.
2. Employees must not share company confidential information with outsiders.
3. Employees must not accept gifts worth more than 1000 rupees from vendors.
4. Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode during meetings.
5. Employees must inform manager if they face any harassment at workplace.
6. Misuse of company laptop for personal business is not allowed.
