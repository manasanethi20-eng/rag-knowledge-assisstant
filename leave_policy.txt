Arrowstack Foods Pvt Ltd - Leave Policy

1. Sick Leave: Every employee gets 12 sick leaves per year.
2. Casual Leave: Every employee gets 12 casual leaves per year.
3. Earned Leave: Every employee gets 15 earned leaves per year.
4. To apply for leave, employee must apply on HR portal at least 2 days before, except for sick leave.
5. Unused sick leaves cannot be carried to next year.
6. If an employee takes more than 3 sick leaves continuously, a doctor certificate is required.
