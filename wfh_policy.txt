Arrowstack Foods Pvt Ltd - Work From Home Policy

1. Employees can work from home for 2 days per week.
2. Work from home days must be approved by manager one day before.
3. Working hours for work from home are 9 AM to 6 PM, same as office.
4. Employees must be available on Teams chat during working hours.
5. Employees working from home must have a stable internet connection.
6. Work from home is not allowed during probation period of first 3 months.
