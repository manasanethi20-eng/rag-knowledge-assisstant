Arrowstack Foods Pvt Ltd - Salary Policy

1. Salary is paid on the 1st of every month.
2. If the 1st is a holiday, salary is paid on the previous working day.
3. Salary slip is sent to employee official email by 3rd of every month.
4. Provident Fund (PF) of 12 percent is deducted from basic salary.
5. Overtime is paid at double rate for work done on Sundays.
6. Annual appraisal and salary hike happens in the month of April.
