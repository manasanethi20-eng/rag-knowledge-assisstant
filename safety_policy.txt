Arrowstack Foods Pvt Ltd - Safety Policy

1. All employees must wear ID card inside office and factory.
2. In case of fire, employees must use stairs, not lift, and gather at assembly point.
3. First aid box is available on every floor near reception.
4. Employees must report any accident to Safety Officer within 1 hour.
5. Smoking is strictly prohibited inside office and factory premises.
6. Emergency contact number for safety is 100 for police and 108 for ambulance.
